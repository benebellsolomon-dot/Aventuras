# Absolute growth rule — cosmology trigger gate (2026-08-22, Ben's ruling)

## The failure (live, story d97724f2 "Amelia of Evermere")
- Turn 45 (19:52Z) was pure dialogue ("why would you want your breasts larger?"). The classifier (kimi-k3) filed it as `beEvents: [{milking i2}, {catalyst i2}]`; the reducer rolled 9 @i3 (fast_metabolizer +1) → **+1 tier**. - Same looseness: the `milking` event was a re-report of an EARLIER drain ("You drained one side") → phantom "drained to 0%, +3 units".
- Root cause: the only growth gate was "the classifier emitted an event of an eligible kind". `beGrowthCosmology` was a prompt HINT (which kind to file the act under), never a condition the engine checked. Ben: *"the BE module has different toggles and states for what causes breast growth; this should always be absolute, no other ways exist, and it should always be followed."*

## The rule
When a story states a growth cosmology, **no growth channel lands unless the story's driving act completed on the page this turn**, for that character. Channels covered: classifier growth events of every kind (catalyst/contact/attempt), guaranteed cast events (`translateSpellEffects`), check-backed growth (`promoteGrowthIntent`, synthesized catalyst), the pity fire (pressure holds at the wall), chronic lactation growth (beats stay banked). Banked `pendingGrowth` earned on an earlier *triggered* turn still lands as the cooldown clears. Stories WITHOUT a cosmology keep the legacy kinds gate, byte-identical.

## Mechanism (whitelist-and-verify, the research/57 lesson applied to the classifier)
- Classifier extension `growthTriggers: [{character, evidence}]` (no hard `.max()`; extractor caps at MAX_BE_EVENTS_PER_TURN, evidence sliced to `BE_TRIGGER_EVIDENCE_MAX` 240; bounded again in classifier-bounds). Instruction lives inside the cosmology block of `buildBeEventInstructions` — absent cosmology = unchanged prompt.
- `be/trigger.ts`: `growthGateRequired(settings)` (cosmology non-empty); `normalizeEvidenceText` (attribute-aware `<pic>` tag removal keeping inner prose, `stripThoughtTags`, style/script blocks dropped, all tags stripped, named + numeric entities decoded with `&amp;` last, zero-width chars dropped, curly quotes/dashes/ellipsis flattened, lowercased); `projectForMatch` (letters/digits/single spaces only — an `<em>` abutting a comma cannot fail an honest quote, and punctuation cannot be used to dodge the check); `verifyGrowthTriggers(triggers, narrative)` → `{verified: Set<normalized name>, rejected: [{character, reason}]}` where a quote must be ≥ 5 words / ≥ 24 chars, must name her (a ≥3-char token of her name) or say she/her/hers/herself, must be a projection-level substring of the page, and must NOT sit entirely inside a line of dialogue (quotes paired by split-parity on `"` — talking about the act is not the act). Reasons: `empty_name | too_short | not_about_her | not_on_page | dialogue_only`.
- Store `applyBeEvents`: verifies once per turn against `narrativeContent` (the finalized text drift reads), resolves verified names to character IDS through the same case-insensitive name lookup the event loops use, logs `growth triggers {proposed, verified, resolved, rejected[, warning]}` whenever the gate is on and there were triggers or growth events (dev-log signature; `warning: no narration text to verify against` when the entry text is empty → fail closed), passes `extras.growthGate = {requireTrigger, triggered}` per girl into the pure reducer.
- Reducer: a verified trigger OUTRANKS the kinds list (a `contact`-filed act on a triggered turn still grows her — the act is the canon statement); untriggered: ambient growth-kind events → `ineligible` with `GROWTH_TRIGGER_BLOCK_NOTE` ("the story's growth act did not complete on the page" — player-visible in the turn log, not a dry beat), EARNED (guaranteed cast/check) events → `banked` (cap-clamped, `GROWTH_TRIGGER_BANK_NOTE`) and land metered on the next triggered turn; step 3's pending land is held on untriggered turns (so a bank never shows on a talk turn — the rule is absolute for banks too); pity fire and chronic supply hold silently (counters kept, capped/pinned) — no per-turn log spam.
- Pre-flight verdict: with a cosmology, only `lands` becomes `conditional` → `[CHECK RESULT]` tells the narrator growth lands ONLY if the act completes on the page (in narration, not dialogue); `blocked_recovery` keeps its "banks, size does NOT visibly change" wording; cap/lock stand. Genre rules gain, only when a cosmology is set, an "absolute rule … the conditional GROWTH line outranks the authorization paragraph" sentence.
- Classifier schema: `growthTriggers` is added to the extended schema ONLY for cosmology stories (`extendClassificationSchemaWithBeEvents(schema, {growthTriggers})`) — cosmology-off prompts byte-identical.
- Settings copy (WritingStyleFields): the cosmology field is labelled as the absolute rule; with a cosmology the event-kind list is ignored.

## What this does NOT make deterministic
The engine still cannot read prose. The classifier can still quote a true sentence that is not the act (any 12-char verbatim substring passes), or miss a real completion (paraphrase instead of quote → growth withheld). The verify step removes the cheapest failure (asserting growth from talk/recall — the quote must exist), and the failure direction flips to "missed growth" which Ben prefers to phantom growth. Review findings + hardening below.

## §magnitude — growth is a cm baseline the story sets; spells/skills/magic modify it (Ben's rulings, 2026-08-22 late)
Rulings: (1) in cosmology stories stop OFFERING growth spells / growth-intent choices, and casts never count as the act; (2) earned power "can build to cause greater growth" — it modifies the next act; (3) every BE system's growth cause has a BASELINE cm amount the story sets, so prose and stats cannot under/over-grow; spells, skills, magic modify that amount.

Model (cosmology stories only; legacy stories unchanged, byte-identical):
- `beGrowthBaselineCm` story setting (Story Settings → Growth Cosmology → "Baseline growth per act (cm of bust)"; unset = `DEFAULT_GROWTH_BASELINE_CM` 2.5 ≈ one cup letter; clamped 0.25–30). `beStoryConfigFromSettings` is the ONE derivation of BE config for the store, CheckPhase and the context builder.
- `be/magnitude.ts`: `tiersForCm(tier, cm, cap)` inverts the natural bust curve (`bustDiffCm`, the cup-letter curve) into whole tiers + a sub-tier `carryCm` (cm per tier ≈ 1.1 at small sizes, ~1.0–1.9 mid); `bonusCmForIntensity` = `SPELL_GROWTH_CM_PER_INTENSITY` 1.5 × intensity (band already folded into intensity by translateSpellEffects: crit +1 / partial −1); `bankBonusCm` capped at `MAX_GROWTH_BONUS_CM` 10; `actGrowthCm` = baseline + bank + carry.
- BodyState: `growthBonusCm` (bank), `growthCarryCm` (carry), `lastGrowth.cm` — present only when non-zero (key-identity kept; schema passthrough + explicit optional fields).
- Reducer (`gated`): growth-kind events never roll. `guaranteed` (cast / check-backed) → `banked` row, bank += bonus cm (a LOCKED girl's cast is muzzled, lock outranks). Ambient events → `ineligible` (untriggered) / `none` "act-driven story" (triggered). Step 6b on a triggered turn: `act` row — tiers = `tiersForCm(tier, baseline + bank + carry, cap)` landed at once (no cooldown — acts are the pacing; lock and cap bind), `lastGrowth.cm` set, bank cleared, carry stored. Pity and chronic-supply never roll in a gated story (silently). Step 3 pending (legacy banks) still lands only on triggered turns.
- Narrator: `[BODY STATE]` per girl gets `ACT GROWTH: if the story's growth act completes for {her} in THIS scene, she grows exactly {cm} cm of bust ({cup} → {cup}) (includes N cm banked from spells/skills) — render that much and no more; if the act does not complete, her size does NOT change.` (computed by the context builder from the same config/state the reducer will use → prose and stats agree by construction). `GROWTH JUST LANDED` directives state the cm when known. Genre rules: "each completed act grows her by exactly the cm the ACT GROWTH line states — spells, skills and magic never grow her directly; they bank into her next act".
- Check layer: `[CHECK RESULT]` verdict `banks_for_act` ("this power BANKS into her next act … her size does NOT change this scene") for any growth cast / growth-intent check in a cosmology story (preview: lock → blocked, cap → at_cap, else banks_for_act); the `conditional` verdict is gone (no producer). Tagging (`buildCheckTaggingInstruction(..., {offerGrowth:false})`): "growth is never an action — never set growthIntent, no growth-spell casts"; the sheet's known-spells line omits growth-effect spells. A typed cast still resolves (essence paid) and banks.
- Turn log: `act` rows ("act growth 2.5 cm (baseline 2.5 + banked 3.0) → +2 tiers, carry 0.3 cm"), `banked` rows ("cast @i2 → +3.0 cm banked into her next act (bank 3.0 cm)").
Defaults to playtest: baseline 2.5 cm/act; 1.5 cm per cast intensity step; bank cap 10 cm. Suite 1524.
