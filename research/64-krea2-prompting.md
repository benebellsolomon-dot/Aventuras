# 64 — Krea 2 Turbo prompting: research, measurements, decisions

**Date:** 2026-08-21 · **Parent:** research/63 (D5 round 1b image-model switch) · **Live profile:** NanoGPT `wavespeed-ai/krea-v2/turbo-lora` + Nikke style / Sagging / NSFW MASTER LoRAs via Civitai token URLs (verified working), style preset `image-style-nikke-krea2`.

## 1. What Krea 2 Turbo is (sourced)

- **Text encoder = Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct**, 12-layer hidden-state tap (`hidden_states[2,5,…,35]`, `(B,12,seq,2560)`) fused inside the DiT by a text-fusion adapter; single-stream DiT, 12.9B, 28 blocks, 3-axis RoPE. Sources: Krea 2 technical report (krea.ai/blog), `krea/Krea-2-Turbo` model card, ComfyUI `comfy/text_encoders/krea2.py`.
- **The encoder is prompted with the Qwen-Image system template:** *"Describe the image by detailing the color, shape, size, texture, quantity, text, spatial relationships of the objects and background:"* — i.e. the model literally reads your prompt as an LLM's description of the finished image. Dense descriptive prose with colors/sizes/quantities/spatial relations is the native format. (ComfyUI `krea2.py`, `KREA2_TEMPLATE`.)
- **No token cap in the reference tokenizer** (`max_length=99999999` in ComfyUI's Qwen3-VL tokenizer); practical limit is attention dilution, not truncation. Community note: reference *images* cost ~1k vision tokens and can starve text — irrelevant to our txt2img path.
- **No negative prompt.** No CFG on Turbo (distilled; `guidance_scale≈0`, **8 steps**). `(word:1.3)` weighting does not isolate a concept — **order is the emphasis lever; the encoder front-loads**. (fal.ai guides, comfy.icu node docs, krea-explorations.)
- **Style:** name the medium/technique concretely and put it last; quality boilerplate ("masterpiece, 8k, best quality") is ignored or harmful. In-image text goes in double quotes. (krea-ai/krea-2 `docs/prompting.md`, fal.ai, InstaSD.)
- **Censorship:** community teardowns place the safety tuning in DiT layers 9–10 and say it damps not only nudity but strong expressions, injury, fear, anger (MyAIForce). Local workflows patch the DiT (Krea2T Enhancer / bypass LoRAs) AND swap an *abliterated* Qwen3-VL encoder. **On NanoGPT we have neither patch** — only LoRAs (NSFW MASTER, NSFW v3) on the diffusion side; the stock encoder still reads the text. Official LoRA guidance: train on Raw, run on Turbo; scale 1.0–1.25 for style, up to 1.5–2.0 for uncensor LoRAs; stack ≤3; rare trigger words used identically. Official LoRA trainer captions: describe literal content only, NO style/medium/lighting-mood words (matches the Nikke LoRA author's "avoid stylizers").
- **Krea's own `expansion.txt`** (the LLM prompt-expander): preserve the user's subjects/actions/colors/spatial arrangement, group each subject with its attributes, quote exact text, honor a requested medium, don't over-specify, output ONE unified paragraph. The Civitai Krea2 workflow's enhancer system prompt (extracted from the workflow JSON subgraph) prescribes: subject (ethnicity+age first, body proportions, face/skin/expression/gaze, hair, accessories, clothing materials/fit) → pose & composition (pose, hands, framing, camera angle, placement, **character dynamics incl. explicit acts**) → environment (location, background, depth of field) → lighting (direction/softness/contrast, time of day) → style & technical (medium, lens look, sharpness) → color & mood — "a single, highly descriptive paragraph of natural, dense prose; concrete, reproducible descriptors".

## 2. Measured here (NanoGPT `krea-v2/turbo`, 1k, two renders)

1. **Long-prompt retention:** a ~1,450-char prompt (≈300 tokens) with five distinctive objects placed at the very END — brass telescope on a tripod right of the window, red paper lantern on the center rafter, black cat on a blue velvet cushion at left, open journal with quill, chessboard beside the oil lamp — rendered **every one**, plus rain on the dormer, lamp, trunks, rug, off-shoulder shirt, mug. Misses: lantern lit (prompt: unlit), gold eyes → grey, "flour on one cheek" absent. **Conclusion: no effective truncation at ~1.5k chars; our 400–700/900 ceilings were leaving detail on the table.**
2. **Two characters with per-person sentences + spatial anchors** ("on the left, …; on the right, …"): every trait stayed on its owner (red wavy hair + green gambeson + brass buckles + boots left; black bob + olive skin + leather halter + grey trousers right; firelit kitchen door behind; wet flagstones). Misses: "fierce grin" rendered as a scowl (consistent with the expression-damping report), "reverse grip" ignored, one extra blade. **Conclusion: the anchored-sentence protocol works; emotion needs to be stated concretely and early.**

## 3. What changed in the app (this commit)

- **Narration-side prose `<pic>` instructions** (`NarrativeService.ts` `INLINE_IMAGE_INSTRUCTIONS_PROSE`, injected as `{{ inlineImageInstructions }}` — code, no pack sync): explains how the encoder reads the prompt; ONE dense paragraph; **count + subjects FIRST** (front-loaded emphasis), then action/interaction, then camera, then environment + light (style appended automatically); no quality words / weights / negatives; quoted in-image text; concrete emotion; anchored per-person sentences kept; breast-band words kept (the BE size-grounding rewrites them and the LLM encoder understands them); **the booru-style rating prefix ("general" / "sensitive" / "explicit, uncensored, detailed anatomy") is gone** — replaced by CONTENT DISCIPLINE: scope clothing/nudity/acts to THIS beat, and when the beat is explicit describe it literally and anatomically in full sentences, no euphemism/slang, no rating labels ("uncensored" is not a picture word and the stock encoder is the one reading it). Density 600–1000 (one character) / 900–1400 (several), hard cap 1600.
- **`image-prompt-analysis` service template** (post-hoc scene path): same encoder note, subject-first order (camera moved after action; style LAST), 600–1000 / max 1400, concrete-emotion and quoted-text rules, beat-scoped content rule 9. **Service template sync v12.**
- Provider side was already right for Turbo: prose models get no `guidance_scale`/`num_inference_steps` (NanoGPT/wavespeed defaults = Turbo's 8-step no-CFG), no negative prompt, `n: 1`, LoRAs pass through.
